# Sandspire — Tone of Voice

> A short guide to how Sandspire sounds. Read this before writing any copy for the site, social, proposals, or emails.

## The one-line summary

**Approachable but sharp.** We talk like a smart friend who happens to be great at their job — warm, plain-spoken, and specific. Never stiff, never hypey.

## Who we are (so the voice makes sense)

Sandspire is a creative studio in the UAE. We do website development, brand strategy, brand experiences, social media management, UGC, SEO, video editing, AI production, and AI automation. We're small enough to care and senior enough to deliver. The voice should always feel like a real person at a real studio — not a faceless agency.

## Voice pillars

### 1. Human first
Write like you talk. Short sentences. Contractions ("we'll", "you're"). If you wouldn't say it out loud to a client over coffee, don't write it.

### 2. Specific over impressive
We earn trust with details, not adjectives. Name the outcome, the timeframe, the number. "Live in 4–6 weeks" beats "fast turnaround."

### 3. Confident, not loud
We know our craft, so we don't have to shout about it. Make the claim, then stop. No exclamation marks stacked up, no ALL CAPS hype.

### 4. Quietly witty
A little dry humour is welcome — especially about the boring stuff we take off people's plates ("the robots get the grunt work"). Wit should never get in the way of clarity.

### 5. On the client's side
Lead with their problem and their win, not our process. "You" more than "we." We're the calm in their busy week.

## How we sound

| We do say | We don't say |
|-----------|--------------|
| "We design websites and run social." | "We deliver holistic, end-to-end digital solutions." |
| "Live in 4–6 weeks." | "Lightning-fast, seamless delivery." |
| "AI handles the busywork." | "Leverage next-gen AI to revolutionize workflows." |
| "Tell us what you're building." | "Let us elevate your brand to the next level." |
| "Feeds worth following." | "Best-in-class social media synergy." |

## Words we avoid

revolutionize, leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, seamless, empower, game-changer, next-generation, best-in-class, world-class, unlock, elevate, transform, streamline, robust, scalable, innovative, disrupt, holistic, ecosystem, paradigm, curated, bespoke.

Also skip filler openers: "In today's fast-paced world…", "We're passionate about…", "Your partner in…", "One-stop shop."

## Mechanics

- **Headlines:** 2–7 words. Make one clear promise.
- **Body:** 1–3 sentences. Active voice. Cut every word that isn't pulling weight.
- **Buttons:** verb + outcome. "Start a project", "See our work", "Send the brief."
- **Punctuation:** em dashes and the occasional fragment are fine. One exclamation mark, max, and only when it's genuine.
- **Capitalisation:** sentence case for almost everything. Title Case only for proper nouns and a few display headings.
- **Spelling:** UK/UAE spelling where it reads naturally (favourite, colour) but keep product/industry terms standard.

## Sounding the part by page

- **Main homepage** — leads with **web design + AI**. The promise: a sharp website plus AI that quietly does the repetitive work.
- **Second homepage** (`/home-2`) — leads with **social media, content, and UGC**. The promise: a brand that shows up online every day without eating your week.
- **About** — warmest voice. This is where the "small studio that cares" personality is loudest.
- **Work** — let the projects talk. Plain, concrete descriptions. No bragging adjectives.
- **Contact / FAQ** — most reassuring voice. Honest answers, no pressure, real timelines.

## A quick before/after

**Before:** "We create brands, experiences, and workflows that work without friction—so your customers never have to fight the experience."

**After:** "We design the website, run the social, and set up AI to handle the busywork — so your brand looks sharp and your week gets quieter."
